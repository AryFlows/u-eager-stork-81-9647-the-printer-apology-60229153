Entry for **The Printer Apology** (make.fun): one funny apology note from an
office printer, 120 characters max.

## How it works

`main.py` writes a single line to `/output/entry.txt` — an apology from a
printer that printed 47 copies of a report after a single click.

## The line

> Sorry I printed 47 copies of your report. You clicked once, I panicked — and nobody here reads past page two anyway.

116 characters — a genuine apology, a printer's own gripe (one click, 47
copies), and the punch lands at the end.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```
